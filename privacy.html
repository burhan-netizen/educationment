Who we are

EducationMent ("we", "us") provides academic support services to university students. For questions about this policy, contact us at educationment.in@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We collect only what we need to deliver your project and support you:

  • Contact details such as your name, email address and WhatsApp number
  • Project details such as your subject, brief, files and deadline
  • Payment confirmation details processed by our payment provider
  • Basic website usage data collected through cookies

How we use your information

We use your information to prepare quotes, deliver your project, communicate with you, process payments and improve our service. We never sell your information.

Confidentiality

Your identity, files and conversations are kept strictly confidential. Experts working on your project see only what they need to complete it.

How long we keep it

We keep project information only as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal obligations, then delete it securely.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To make a request, contact us at educationment.in@gmail.com.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to run the website and optional analytics cookies to understand how it is used. You can control cookies through your browser settings.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
